One sentence to convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun, entered in make.fun's 'A Day of One-Line Pitches' bounty. The sentence is the whole entry; the program writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You know that app idea you keep describing over dinner? Say it to an AI agent on make.fun in plain words - it builds the app while you watch, you just try it and say what to fix. No code, no hiring, no months of waiting.

It speaks to one specific friend and one specific idea, gives a concrete reason to say yes now, and keeps the risk at zero: you only ever describe, try and comment.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
